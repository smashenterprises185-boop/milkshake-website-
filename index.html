Since day one

Blended by hand. On purpose.

Whisk & Wave started as a single blender on a market stall. We got tired of shakes that were really just flavored ice, so we built ours the slow way: real fruit macerated overnight, dairy from a farm twenty minutes out, and a blend time short enough that it never turns to soup.

Every glass is made when you order it — not scooped from a tub. That's the whole recipe.
